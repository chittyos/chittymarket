# Property lead decision rules

## Authority labels

Classify each input as:
1. **Verified portfolio fact** — signed agreement, payment record, property record, or canonical portfolio record.
2. **Verified communication fact** — supported by a specific message and retained identifier.
3. **Market heuristic** — supported by current market evidence and periodically revalidated.
4. **Inference** — useful for triage but insufficient for commitment, rejection, or outbound representation.

Signed terminations, executed leases, and superseding agreements control over automated reminders and older correspondence.

## Lead-state rules

- Deduplicate by person and platform channel.
- Reconcile the newest substantive message before ranking.
- A withdrawal or accepted alternative property closes the lead until reengagement.
- Direct property engagement outranks an area-wide match when other factors are comparable.
- Do not call a lead booked without the portfolio's required agreement and payment state.
- Keep competing inquiries separate from completed commitments.

## Availability

Establish availability from executed leases, signed terminations, possession dates, and realistic turnover windows. Distinguish `may work`, `held`, and `contracted` dates. Same-day turnover is a dependency, not assumed availability.

## Seasonality

Use seasonality only as a periodically revalidated market heuristic. Never use it as a tenant-selection criterion or guarantee. Compare expected vacancy, turnover cost, and probability-weighted revenue rather than relying on a calendar-month rule alone.

## Pricing and qualification

Confirm rent, prorations, utilities, fees, deposit, parking, pet charges, and any temporary-accommodation allocation. A budget below the offered rate is a conflict, not an automatic rejection. Reconcile narrative text with structured platform fields and leave unresolved conflicts explicit.

## Scoring

Use internal scores only when a ranked comparison materially helps:
- Conversion: intent/engagement, recency/responsiveness, material-term alignment, readiness, unresolved uncertainty.
- Economic/operational: probability-weighted revenue/term, vacancy quality, turnover burden, property/building fit, qualification certainty.

Label scores as decision support, never eligibility or screening scores. Never use protected characteristics.
