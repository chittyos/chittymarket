# Furnished-Condos.com Design

Use the bundled brand guide and tokens as authoritative implementation references.

## Required workflow

1. Read `references/brand-guide.md` for the relevant surface and constraints.
2. Read `references/tokens.md` when exact color, type, spacing, radius, shadow, or material values matter.
3. Use `assets/brand/f-canonical-mark.svg` directly for the canonical `[ ƒ ]` mark.
4. Preserve the SVG paths, viewBox, internal whitespace, proportions, optical centering, and native artwork colors. Never redraw, trace, normalize, crop, recolor, or reconstruct the mark from a font glyph.
5. Treat any mark+wordmark horizontal composition as **PROPOSED — NOT CANONICAL** unless the brand owner explicitly promotes it.
6. Keep vector identity assets vector through final export whenever the target format supports it.
7. For raster output, render the canonical SVG at sufficient resolution without changing geometry and verify that no ascender, descender, bracket, or whitespace is clipped.
8. For QR/signage work, generate the QR separately from the visual design, preserve its quiet zone and contrast, and validate the final composited output with an actual decoder before delivery.
9. For light/dark variants, change the surrounding composition; do not mutate the canonical mark. If a reversed canonical asset is not supplied, do not invent one. Place the approved mark on a compatible local panel/surface.
10. Before handoff, visually QA alignment, spacing, clipping, contrast, print size, source fidelity, and final QR readability.

## Non-negotiable visual rules

- Default hierarchy: Warm Ivory → Graphite → Slate → Charcoal → Slate Ink.
- Natural Brass/Bronze are restrained material accents, never the primary CTA or a recurring decorative theme.
- Use Inter for operational/UI text. Reserve Cormorant Garamond for approved editorial/manifesto moments.
- Prefer hairline separators, flat surfaces, restrained radii, and generous negative space.
- Avoid gradients, glassmorphism, glow, chunky shadows, excessive pills, oversized radii, invented accent colors, generic SaaS-card styling, stock-hospitality aesthetics, and emoji.
- Voice is clear, structured, calm, factual, trust-forward, and benefit-led; avoid hype.

## Source classification

Treat `assets/brand/f-canonical-mark.svg` as **CANONICAL / IMMUTABLE**. Treat proposed lockups, AI material renders, and exploratory compositions as references only unless explicitly approved by the brand owner.
