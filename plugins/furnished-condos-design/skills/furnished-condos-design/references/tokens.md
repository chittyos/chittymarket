## Typography

- Inter: operational UI, forms, tables, pricing, dashboards, documents, signage instructions.
- Cormorant Garamond: only approved editorial/manifesto moments such as `house [ ƒ ] home`.

## Geometry

- 4px base spacing grid.
- Radius: 0px paper/data; 2px structure; 4px controls; 8px largest normal UI radius; pill only for genuine tags/status.
- Flat by default. Hairline borders and tonal separation do the work.
- One subtle ordinary shadow tier; deeper material shadow only for selected identity moments.

## Materiality

Material treatments may evoke plaster, limestone, cotton paper, shallow relief, and soft daylight shadow, but they must not change canonical mark geometry.

## Prohibited

No gradients as brand language, glassmorphism, glow, chunky shadows, excessive pills, oversized rounded corners, invented accent colors, generic SaaS defaults, or stock-hospitality styling.
