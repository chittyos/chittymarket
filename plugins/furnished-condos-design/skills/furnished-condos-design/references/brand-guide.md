# Furnished-Condos.com Brand Guide — Operational Reference

This reference is distilled from the supplied **Furnished-Condos.com Design System** bundle. Preserve its classifications.

## Brand posture

Furnished-Condos.com is a mid-term furnished housing brand and verified reference index. The visual north star is: **less software, more architecture; less interface, more object; less branding, more artifact.**

Voice is clear, structured, professional, calm, factual, trust-forward, and benefit-led. Avoid hype and emoji.

## Canonical mark

The mark is `[ ƒ ]` — one unified symbol. The brackets represent prepared space; the ƒ is the brand philosophy inhabiting it.

Canonical asset: `assets/brand/f-canonical-mark.svg`.

Rules:
- Never redraw, recolor, crop, trace, normalize, or recenter.
- Never change paths, proportions, viewBox, internal whitespace, or optical centering.
- Never substitute a font glyph where SVG placement is possible.
- Native artwork colors are ƒ `#0B141A`, brackets `#6F7880`.
- No canonical horizontal lockup exists. Any mark+wordmark composition is **PROPOSED — NOT CANONICAL** until explicitly approved.

Two identity layers are allowed:
- **Production/vector:** flat, exact, deterministic.
- **Material/application:** restrained physical texture, shallow relief, soft daylight shadow; geometry never changes.

## Color doctrine

Use Warm Ivory → Graphite → Slate → Charcoal → Slate Ink as the normal hierarchy. Brass/Bronze are optional material accents only and should remain scarce.

Never use parchment/yellow ivory, generic blue or green “trust” hues, gradients, glow, glassmorphism, or decorative metallic fills.

## Typography

Inter is the general typeface. Cormorant Garamond is a narrow editorial exception only.

## Layout

Use a 4px spacing grid, generous negative space, strong alignment, and hairline separators. Avoid inflating every section into a rounded card.

## Print and signage

- Preserve canonical SVG as vector through the print pipeline whenever possible.
- Respect the SVG viewBox and full safe bounds; never trim by visible-pixel heuristics.
- For laser-printer output, prioritize high contrast, clean vector edges, and restrained texture that survives monochrome conversion.
- Do not invent a reversed canonical mark. For dark compositions, place the unchanged canonical mark on an approved light/local panel when needed.
- QR codes are functional assets, not decoration. Generate separately, keep adequate quiet zone and contrast, composite without distortion, then scan-validate the final export.
- Verify physical dimensions, clipping, optical alignment, and final print scaling at 100% / Actual Size.

## Preserved copy

Do not rephrase:
- `FURNISHED-CONDOS.COM | stay ready.`
- `house [ ƒ ] home`
- `we furnished the space between.`
- `design · furnish · modernize · maintain · manage · market · lease`
- `The homes became more individual. The operation became more systematic.`
